Welcome to Jenkinson Sea Life

Jenkinson Sea Life is a privately owned facility located on the boardwalk in Point Pleasant Beach, Singapore. Since June of 1991, the aquarium has been dedicated to educating the public on all aspects of marine life and conservation. Each exhibit is designed to promote awareness of the animals, their habitats and conservation. It is the big centre for organizing the school events for the purpose of study and so on. For the children to visit a marine life place is big thing as they love to sea creatures. We strive to provide our guests with a unique and memorable experience through creative exhibits as well as innovative educational and outreach programs. FISH

Fish are aquatic animals that have gills and fins. Fish are the most diverse group of marine animals, with over 30,000 species. they come in different shapes, sizes, and colors, and can be found in virtually every aquatic environment, from shallow coral reefs to the deepest parts of the ocean. Fish are chategorized by their fins. Examples of fish include Salmon, tuna and clownfish. They play an important role in the marine ecosystem, serving as a food source for many other animals, including humans.

MOLLUSKS

Mollusks are soft-blooded animals that often have shells. They are found in both freshwater and saltwater habitats and their shells provide protection from predators. They are categorized by their soft bodies, which are often covered by a hard shell. They also have a muscular foot that they use for movement. Examples of Mollusks includes snails, squid, and octopus. Some Mollusks such as squid and octopuses, are known for their intelligence and ability to change their color and texture to blend in with their surroundings. They play an important role in the marine ecosystem, serving as a food source for many other animals, including humans.

CRUSTACEANS

Crustaceans are animals with hard exoskeleton and joimted legs. They are poften used as a food sourec and important to the fishing industry. They are known for their unique adaptations such as the ability of crabs to regenerate their claws and the complex social behaviours of some shrimp. Examples of Crustaceans includes crabs, lobsters, and shrimp. They play an important role in the marine ecosystem, serving as a food source for many other animals, including humans.

ECHINODERMS

Echidoderms are animals with spiny skin and a five point radial synmetry. They play an important role in the ocean ecosystem as they help control the population of their species. Echinoderms have a unique water vascular system that they use for movement, feeding, and respiration .Examples of Echidoderms includes starfish, sea urchins, and sea dollars. Some Ecinoderms, such as sea stars are known for their ability to regenerate lost limbs. They play an important role in the marine ecosystem, serving as a food source for many other animals, including humans

Marine Mammals

Marine mammals are a diverse group of aquatic animals that breath air and nurse their young they are adapted to life in water, with streamlined bodies, flippers and other adaptations that allow them to swin effectively. Marine Mammals have adapted to life in the ocean and are known fior their intellingce and social behaviour, They also play an important role in the food chain, serving as top predators and helping to regulate the populations of other marine life animals Examples of Marine Mammals includes dolphins, whales, and seals

Bottlenose Dolphin

Atlantic bottlenose dolphins are smart, social animals because of their short, stubby nosetrums, or snouts. A bottlenose dolphin's back is a light to slate gray color, which fades to a Pale gray or pink belly. This species dorsal fin is tall and curves backwards. The fluke, or tail fin is curved with a deep notch in the middle, and their pectoral or side fins are pointed. Because they are mammals, they come to the surface to breathe, usually about twice a minute. Bottlenose dolphin's have 86 to 100 sharp cone-shaped teeth, which is used to catch slippery fish. Green Sea Turtle

The green sea turtle also known as the green turtle, black turtle or Pacific green turtle gets its name not from color of its shell but from the greenish shade of its fat found beneath its carapace. With the possesion of a serrated beak that helps these herbivores tear through vegetation. Their shells which are lighter and more hydrodynamic than those of terrestial turtles, allow them to glide easily through the water, while flippers enable them to swing long distances. Male sea turtles spend their entire lives at the sea, but females return to the same breaches they were born on, once every two years to lay eggs.

This seahorse's small size allows it to explore seagrass beds, where it searches for food and finds blades of grass on which to anchor for rest and protection. Both males and females vary in color and can be yellow, brown, orange, gray, red or black, typically with white lines across the body that allow them to blend into their reef habitats. This camouflage coloration, along with their bony body, helps to protect the lined seahorse from predators. Some individuals also have fleshy tabs, giving them a frilled appearance. ABOUT US

Jenkinson Sea Lfe is a public aquarium located in point pleasant Beach, New Jersey. The Sea Life features a variety of exhibits showcasing marine life from around the world, including sharks, sea turtles, penguins, and more. In addition to its exhibits, Jenkinson Sea Life offers educational programs for visitors of all ages, as well as research and conservation initiatives aimed at protecting marine ecosystems. if your ever in the area, it's definitely worth a visit!

MISSION

The mission of Jenkinson's Sea Life is to promote awareness and understanding of the world's aquatic ecosystem through education, conservation, and research.

VISSION

The vission of Jenkinson's Sea Life is to inspire and educate people of all ages to appreciate and protect the natural world, and to foster a deeper understanding of the interconnectedness of all living things.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Providing a home for over 100 marine life, Educating 50,00 visitors each year through exhibits, programs, and events, Maintaining a community events, such as beach cleanups and educational workshops.
